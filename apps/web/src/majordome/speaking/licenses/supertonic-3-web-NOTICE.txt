Supertonic 3 voice model

The Mac app and the web app download the model's files on first use, through
https://majord.link/voice/. Where the graphics card has 16-bit floats, the
vector estimator and the vocoder are files Majordome converted to 16-bit
floating point from the ones Supertone Inc. published at
https://huggingface.co/supertone-oss-archive/supertonic-3 (release
https://github.com/Wozacosta/majordome/releases/tag/voice-supertonic-3-web-1);
elsewhere they are the int8 conversion made by k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
(https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx, release
https://github.com/Wozacosta/majordome/releases/tag/voice-supertonic-3-int8-1).
The weights are otherwise unchanged. They are used under the BigScience Open
RAIL-M License below. The use restrictions in its Attachment A apply to
everyone who uses the voice. Supertone Inc. does not endorse Majordome.
