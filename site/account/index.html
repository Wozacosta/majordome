How signing in works

A Majordome account does one thing: it tells a new device where your servers are, so you never scan a code. Your notes never pass through it.

The whole picture

  • 1You sign in with Google or your email.
  • 2The account hands back your server list and a pass that lasts a minute.
  • 3Your device shows that pass to the server it wants to open.
  • 4The server checks the pass itself, then connects the device like a scanned code would.

On your Mac

Majordome app, bottom of the sidebar

  1. Press Sign inUse Google or your email.
  2. Your computers joinEvery computer and server the app is connected to is added to your account.
  3. Give the Mac an addressOne button, only if you want your phone to reach this Mac. A hosted server already has one.
  4. DoneYour account now knows where your servers are.

On your iPhone

Majordome app, first screen

  1. Press Sign inThe same account as on your Mac.
  2. See your serversThe phone lists them by name.
  3. Press ConnectNo code to scan, nothing to type.
  4. You are inToday, your chats and your notes are there.

In any browser

On a borrowed computer, or a phone without the app

  1. Open majord.me/openOne address to remember.
  2. Sign inGoogle or your email.
  3. Press OpenNext to the server you want.
  4. You are inThe browser lands on your server, signed in.

With no computer to leave on

Majordome hosts the server, by invitation

  1. Open majord.me/startSign in first.
  2. Enter your invite codeThat is all it asks.
  3. Wait half a minuteA private server is made for you.
  4. It is yoursIt belongs to your account from its first second, so losing a device never locks you out.

What each part knows

Your account Your email, how you sign in, and the name and address of each of your servers.
Each server Which account it belongs to, plus your notes, your conversations and your AI sign-in. These never leave it.
Your device That it is signed in, and a key to each server it is connected to.
Majordome, the project Nothing about your notes. For a hosted server or a phone address, see Privacy.

Good to know

An account is optional. Scanning the code your computer shows still works, with no account at all. Whoever can sign in to your account can open your servers, so protect it as you would your email. To take a computer out, open Settings, then Your account, and press Remove from account: every device that got in by signing in is signed out of it.
