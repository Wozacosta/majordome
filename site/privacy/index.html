Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

The short version

Majordome is an open-source app that runs on your own computer or server. Your notes and conversations stay there. The app sends no usage statistics and no crash reports. This site counts page views without cookies and has no advertising.

A few optional services need a little information to work. Each is listed below with what it keeps. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising or to train AI models.

What stays on your machines

  • Your notes, their history, and your private documents.
  • Your conversations with the assistant.
  • Your sign-ins to AI providers, and any passwords or keys you save in the app.

"Your machines" means the computer Majordome is installed on and any server you connect it to. Majordome, the project, never receives these.

The AI you use

When you talk to the assistant, your message and the notes it reads go from your machine to the AI provider you signed in to, such as Anthropic or OpenAI, under your own account and that provider's terms. They do not pass through Majordome.

Optional services and what each keeps

A Majordome account

An account lets a new device find your servers when you sign in. It is run by Clerk, which keeps:

  • your email address and how you sign in;
  • if you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture, as Google gives them;
  • the list of your servers: a name and an address for each;
  • a record of your sign-ins, to keep the account safe.

Signing in stores cookies in your browser so you stay signed in. Each of your servers keeps which account it belongs to. An account never holds your notes.

Signing in with Google

Majordome asks Google only for your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to identify your account. They are not shared with anyone else and are not used for anything else.

Backing up to Google Drive or GitHub

If you turn on a backup, your notes go from your machine straight to your own Google Drive or GitHub account. With Google Drive, Majordome can reach only the files it created there. The permission is kept on your machine, not by the project.

An address for your phone

If you give your computer an address under majord.link, the address service keeps a fingerprint of your computer's key, the address, when it was made, and the IP address that asked for it, which limits abuse. An address unused for 14 days is removed. Traffic to that address passes through Cloudflare on its way to your computer.

A server Majordome hosts for you

By invitation or for a monthly price, Majordome can run your server. It then holds your notes, conversations and AI sign-in, on a machine in Paris rented from Fly.io, with daily copies of its disk kept for 30 days so it can be restored. The service also keeps your invite code, the server's name and the IP address that asked for it. Ask for the server to be removed and its disk and copies are deleted.

Paying for a server

If you pay on this site, the payment is taken by Stripe, which keeps your email address, your payment method, your country and your invoices. If you buy in the iPhone app, Apple takes the payment and tells Majordome only that a subscription exists. Majordome never sees your card. It keeps which account pays, through which of the two, whether the subscription is current, and until when. The terms say what happens when a subscription ends.

News of Linux and Windows

If you leave your email on this site to hear when Majordome runs on Linux or Windows, the project keeps that email, which of the two you chose, when you asked, and the IP address you asked from, which limits abuse. It is used for that one message and nothing else, and is stored with Cloudflare.

Notifications on your phone

Notifications are sent by your own server through your phone's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla), which delivers them without being able to read them.

This site and downloads

This site is served by Vercel and the app is downloaded from GitHub. Like any web host, they see your IP address when you visit or download. The app checks GitHub for updates.

This site counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics: which page was opened, the site that linked to it, and the country, browser and kind of device. It sets no cookie, keeps no IP address and does not follow you from one day to the next or to other sites. Only the page's path is counted, never an invite code or anything else in the address. The app itself is not counted at all.

Seeing, changing or deleting your data

Everything on your own machines is yours to read, change or delete at any time: it is plain files. To delete your Majordome account, a majord.link address, a hosted server or your email on the Linux and Windows list, or to get a copy of what a service keeps about you, write to privacy@majord.me. It is done within 30 days. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.
