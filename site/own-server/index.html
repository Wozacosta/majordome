Your own server

Majordome on a Linux server you rent, so it keeps working while your computer is off.

What you need

  • A Linux server with at least 2 GB of memory, from any company that rents them. The smallest one that fits costs about €5 a month, paid to that company. Majordome charges nothing.
  • A free Tailscale account. Tailscale keeps the server private: nothing on it is reachable from the internet, only from your own devices.
  • Your own Claude plan. You sign in to it on the server.

Set it up

  1. Make a Tailscale key on Tailscale's keys page, and turn on HTTPS Certificates on its DNS page.
  2. Run one command on the server, as root, with your own time zone so routines follow your clock: 
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Wozacosta/majordome/main/deploy/install.sh | TZ=Europe/Paris sh
    It asks for the key, installs what is missing, starts Majordome, makes it update itself every night, and ends with a link. Running it again is safe, and gives a new link.
  3. Connect. In Majordome on your computer, open Settings, then Your servers, press Add a computer and paste the link. Then press Move my notes to your server.
  4. Sign in to Claude on the server, in its Settings, under AI accounts: open the link it shows, approve, and paste back the code Claude gives you.
  5. Set up your phone from Phone & notifications on your computer, with nothing to install on the phone.

With a phone and no computer

It works, but it was built for someone who also has a computer:

  • Install the Tailscale app on the phone and sign in to the same account, so the phone can reach the server.
  • Open the link the command prints on the phone, or paste it in Add a computer in the Majordome app.
  • There are no notes to move. The server starts with an empty notes folder.
  • Sign in to Claude from the phone, in the server's Settings, under AI accounts.
