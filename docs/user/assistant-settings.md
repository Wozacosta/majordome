# Setting Majordome up by asking

The assistant beside every screen can look at how Majordome is set up and change most settings for
you. Ask in plain words:

> Is my backup working?
>
> Are my computer and my server in step?
>
> Let routines work for up to 45 minutes.
>
> Remove the Fitness topic.

## What it does at once

Backing up now, turning backup syncing on, and adding, renaming or reordering topics.

## What it asks you first

Anything that is hard to take back, or that lets it do more on its own: changing the routine
limits, turning backup syncing off, removing the folder backup,
disconnecting Google Drive, forgetting the GitHub sign-in, removing a topic, deleting a private
document, and moving routines to this computer.

A card appears in the conversation: **Majordome wants to: …**, with what it means for you. Nothing
changes until you press **Allow**. **Don't allow** closes it, and the assistant does not ask again.
You also get a notification, and the question shows under **Needs you** in **What it did**. A question
nobody answers closes by itself after 24 hours, with nothing changed.

## What stays with you

Signing in (GitHub, Google, Claude or another AI), passphrases, adding a private document, setting
up a phone, your server, and letting a device get notifications. The assistant tells you
which screen to open. It never asks for a password, a key or a code in the conversation; if it
does, don't give one.
