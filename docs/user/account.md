# Your account

A Majordome account is optional. It does one thing: a device you sign in on finds your servers
by itself, with no code to scan or type. Pairing with a code keeps working, and nothing else in
Majordome needs an account. A server Majordome hosts for you is the exception: it belongs to
the account you made it with.

## Sign in

Press **Sign in** at the bottom of the sidebar, or in **Settings > Your account**, and use
Google or your email. Every computer and server the app is connected to joins your account,
and the app opens **Settings > Your account**, which shows each of them. If one still reads
**Not in your account yet**, press **Add to my account** next to it.

A computer can only be found by your other devices once it has an address outside your home.
A server Majordome hosts already has one. For your own computer, **Settings > Your account**
says so and has a **Give it an address** button: it puts the computer on a public address that
only devices signed in to your account, or paired with a code, can open.

## Open Majordome on another device

- **In a browser, such as on your phone:** open <https://majord.me/open> and sign in. With one
  server in your account it opens by itself; with several, press **Open** next to the one you
  want.
- **In the Majordome app on another computer:** open **Settings > Your account**, sign in, and
  press **Connect** next to the server under **Also in your account**.

If the page says there is nothing to open, the computer has no address yet: see
[Sign in](#sign-in). If a server does not answer, check that it is on and connected to the
internet.

## Sign out on a phone

**Sign out** also disconnects the phone from every computer, so no notes or chats stay on it
for whoever uses it next. Nothing is deleted on the computers. Sign in again and press
**Connect** to bring back a computer in your account; one you had added with a code needs a
new code.

## Remove a computer from your account

Press **Remove from account** on the same page. Every device that got in by signing in is
signed out of that computer; devices paired with a code stay. A server Majordome hosts keeps
its account: it was set when the server was made.

## What is kept, and where

- The account service (Clerk) keeps your email and how you sign in.
- Your account keeps the list of your servers: a name and an address each.
- Each server keeps which account it belongs to. Your notes and conversations stay on your
  servers; signing in only lets a device connect to them.

Anyone who can sign in to your account can open your servers, so protect it as you would your
email.
