# Routines and reminders

A routine is something Majordome does for you again and again, such as "Start my day" every
morning. A reminder happens once. You set both up by telling the assistant what you want.

## Add a routine

Open **Routines**. The list is on one side and the assistant on the other; on a phone, tap **Set up
with the assistant**. Say what you would like done and when:

> Every Friday evening, list what I spent this week.

The assistant asks what it still needs to know, one question at a time: what exactly to do, when,
and what you want back (a note, a line in your feed, a notification). It then says the routine back
to you in one sentence and waits for your yes before it creates it. The new routine appears in the
list at once.

Times are in your own time, by the clock of the machine that runs your routines.

## Change, pause, try or delete one

Each routine shows what it does, when it runs next and how its last run went.

- The switch turns it off and on again.
- **Run now** tries it right away. This works even when it is off.
- **Edit** hands it to the assistant: say what should be different. It tells you what will change
  and waits for your yes.
- The bin deletes it, after asking. A default routine you delete does not come back.

What a routine did is in the **Feed**, where **Undo this** takes a run back.

## Reminders

Ask in any conversation, not only on the Routines screen:

> Remind me on Friday at 9 to call the bank.

When the time comes, Majordome sends a notification and posts in the conversation where you asked.
Upcoming reminders are listed under **Reminders** on the Routines screen, and the next few under
**Coming up** on **Today**. **Cancel** removes one. To move a reminder, cancel it and ask again.

## Good to know

- With an [always-on server](./always-on-server.md), routines run there, and the Routines screen
  talks to that machine. The assistant in a conversation on another machine can set a reminder but
  not a routine; it tells you to use the Routines screen instead.
- **Limits**, at the bottom of the list, stop a run that takes too long and cap how many runs start
  in a day. Reminders always go off and do not count toward that number.
- The conversation beside the list keeps what you set up earlier. **Start over** begins a new one;
  the old one moves to the archive, under Settings.
