# Skills

A skill is a way of doing one job, written down step by step, that your assistant follows by name:
how your receipts are filed, how your weekly review goes. Majordome comes with a few, and your
assistant can write new ones for itself as it works with you.

## Teaching one

Explain how you want something done and say it should stay that way: "from now on", "every time",
"remember how to do this". The assistant writes the steps down and tells you a skill is waiting.
The nightly "Tidy my memory" routine does the same when your notes describe a way of doing
something that keeps coming back.

## Nothing is used without your yes

A skill steers every later conversation and routine, including the ones that run while you are
away. So a skill your assistant wrote is not used until you keep it.

Open **Settings > Skills**. Under **Waiting for your yes**, choose **Read it** to see the exact
steps, then **Keep** or **Delete**. A new version of a skill you already have says that it replaces
the one in use.

This holds whatever the assistant was told: at the end of every conversation and routine, anything
written straight into the skills folder is moved back to the waiting list, and a skill it deleted is
put back.

## Stop using one

**Stop using** on a skill moves it back to the waiting list. **Keep** brings it back; **Delete**
removes it. Each decision is saved in your notes' history.

## Where they live

Skills are files in your notes folder, under `.agents/skills`, one folder each with a `SKILL.md`.
They are backed up and synced with your notes, and work with every AI you sign in to. Ones that wait
are under `.agents/proposed-skills`.
