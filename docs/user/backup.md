# Backing up and restoring your notes

Your notes and their whole history live in one folder on your computer. **Settings > Back up my
brain** keeps a second copy somewhere else, and brings it back when you need it.

## Where the copies can go

One place is enough. The top of the page says whether your notes are backed up and when the last
copy was made.

- **Backup folder**: the quickest, with no other account. Every night Majordome puts a locked copy
  in a folder you choose, such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox or a USB drive. iCloud Drive, Dropbox,
  OneDrive and Google Drive get a button of their own when they are on the computer; **Choose
  folder** picks any other place.
- **Google Drive**: the same locked copy, sent every night to a "Majordome backups" folder in your
  Drive.
- **GitHub**: for people who already have a GitHub account. A private copy on your account, updated
  a few minutes after every change, with no passphrase.

The folder and Google Drive copies are locked with a passphrase: a password only for your backups.
You type it twice, and Majordome never shows it again, so write it down on paper. Without it, nobody
can open the copies, including you.

A nightly copy that was missed because the computer was off is made the next time it is on.
**Back up now** makes one straight away.

Stopping a backup never deletes the copies that were already made.

## Restore from a backup

Use this on a new computer, or after losing your notes. You need a copy and your passphrase.

1. On the welcome screen choose **Restore from a backup**, or open **Settings > Back up my brain**
   and choose **Restore from a backup** at the bottom.
2. Choose the folder your copies are in. The newest one is used. For a copy in Google Drive,
   download the newest file from the "Majordome backups" folder first, then choose the folder you
   saved it in.
3. Type your passphrase. It is used once and not kept.
4. Choose **Restore my notes**.

Your notes come back in a new folder called Majordome in your home folder; **Put them somewhere
else** picks another place. Nothing that is already on the computer is changed: if a Majordome
folder is there, the new one is called Majordome 2. Backups are not set up on the restored notes;
turn one on again in **Back up my brain**.

Notes backed up to GitHub are not restored from this screen yet.

The copies can also be opened without Majordome; **Without Majordome**, under the restore form,
shows how.
