# Reading list

The reading list holds what you want to read, watch or listen to later: articles, videos, papers,
podcasts. Open **Reading list** in the sidebar.

## Save something

Paste a link in the field at the top and press Enter. Majordome looks up its title, its picture,
where it is from and, when it can tell, how long it takes. The picture is loaded from the page's
own site each time the list is shown.

A few seconds after you save a link, your AI account adds up to three tags and one sentence on what
it is about. It prefers tags you already use, and never changes tags you put there yourself. This
uses a little of your AI plan each time; without an AI account ready, the link is simply saved
without them. Something without a link, such as a book a friend
recommended, can be saved by typing its title instead. A link you already saved is not added twice.

## Keep it in order

- Tick the box on the right when you have finished something. Finished rows are hidden; turn off
  **Hide finished** to see them again, and untick one to bring it back.
- Drag a row by its handle to put what matters most on top. Dragging is off while a kind or tag is
  chosen.
- Press **+** under a title to add a tag, and **×** on a tag to take it off. The chips above the
  list show only one kind or one tag at a time.
- The star marks what you would recommend. The **…** menu renames a row, changes its length, adds a
  note in your own words, or removes it; a removal can be undone right after.

The line above the list adds up the time left across everything unfinished that has a length.

## Ask the assistant

**Ask the assistant about this** in a row's **…** menu writes the question in the assistant's box
for you to send. Before you have read something, it asks what it covers and whether it is worth
your time. Once it is finished, it asks for a short quiz and saves what you want to remember as the
row's note.

## Good to know

The list lives in your notes folder, so it is backed up with your notes. Majordome never loads
pictures from the sites you save.
