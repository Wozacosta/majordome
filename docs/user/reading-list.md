# Reading list

The reading list holds what you want to read, watch or listen to later: articles, videos, papers,
podcasts. Open **Reading list** in the sidebar.

## Save something

Paste a link in the field at the top and press Enter. Majordome looks up its title, its picture,
where it is from and, when it can tell, how long it takes: the reading time of an article, the
playing time of a video or podcast. The picture is loaded from the page's
own site each time the list is shown.

A few seconds after you save a link, your AI account adds up to three tags and one sentence on what
it is about. It prefers tags you already use, and never changes tags you put there yourself. This
uses a little of your AI plan each time; without an AI account ready, the link is simply saved
without them. Something without a link, such as a book a friend
recommended, can be saved by typing its title instead. A link you already saved is not added twice.

## Next or Everything

The switch at the top chooses what you see, and is remembered on this device.

- **Next** shows only the first three things on your list that are neither finished nor snoozed,
  with three answers each: **Finished**; **Done a bit**, which asks where you got to, keeps that on
  the row and snoozes it until tomorrow; or **Not now**, which only snoozes it until tomorrow. The
  line under them says how much more is waiting.
- **Everything** is the whole list, with its filters and its order to arrange.

## Keep it in order

- Tick the box on the right when you have finished something. Finished rows are hidden; turn off
  **Hide finished** to see them again, and untick one to bring it back.
- The search field finds rows by any words in their title, site, tags, summary, notes or link.
- Drag a row by its handle to put what matters most on top. Dragging is off while a search, a kind
  or a tag narrows the list.
- Press **+** under a title to add a tag, and **×** on a tag to take it off. The chips above the
  list show only one kind or one tag at a time.
- The star marks what you would recommend. The **…** menu renames a row, changes its length or its
  kind (article, video, podcast…), adds a note in your own words or a line on why to read it, or
  removes it.
- A removed row goes to the folded **Trash** group under the list. **Put back** returns it to its
  place; **Delete for good** cannot be undone. Saving a link that is in the Trash brings it back.
- **Look it up again** in the **…** menu reads the link once more. Use it on a video saved before
  Majordome could tell its length.
- A link saved without a picture, because its page was down or refused to be read, is looked up
  again by itself each time Majordome starts. The picture shows the next time you open the list.

The line above the list adds up the time left across everything unfinished that has a length.

## Snooze what can wait

**Snooze…** in a row's **…** menu hides it until tomorrow, next week or in a month. Snoozed rows
wait in the folded **Snoozed** group under the list, each with the day it is back, and are not
counted in the time left. On that day the row returns to its place by itself. To bring one back
sooner, open the group and choose **Wake it now** in its **…** menu. Finishing a row also ends its
snooze.

## Ask the assistant

The assistant can read and change the list for you in any conversation: "save this for later",
"what should I read next", "I finished the compilers article, note that the parsing part was the
useful one", or "find me five things on Rust and add them". When it recommends something it
writes the **Why** line on the row. It only marks something finished, or moves it to the Trash,
when you say so, and it refuses a link whose site says the page does not exist.

**Ask the assistant about this** in a row's **…** menu writes the question in the assistant's box
for you to send. Before you have read something, it asks what it covers and whether it is worth
your time. Once it is finished, it asks for a short quiz and saves what you want to remember as the
row's note.

## Good to know

The list lives in your notes folder, so it is backed up with your notes. Majordome never loads
pictures from the sites you save.
